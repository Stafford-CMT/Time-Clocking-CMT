Time Slide · guide for tablet users

Clocking your time on a tablet

Everything you need to clock on and off contracts, and to put your day right, using your finger. You only need two screens: the Dashboard and Edit or Commit times. Anything else is done on a laptop.

1Getting started

  1. Hold the tablet sideways (landscape). Time Slide is laid out for that.
  2. Open Microsoft Edge and go to timeslide.cliffeng.com.
  3. Sign in with your normal Clifford work account. The tablet is yours, so you'll stay signed in.
  4. Tip: in Edge's menu (⋯ at the bottom of the screen), choose Add to phone or Add to home screen. Time Slide then opens from an icon like any other app.

2The four finger moves

TapTouch and let go. Presses buttons and opens a block of time.
SwipeSlide straight away. Scrolls the page, the timeline or the day strip.
Hold, then slideKeep your finger still until the tablet buzzes, then slide. Moves a tile or draws time.
PinchTwo fingers apart or together. Zooms a tile's picture.

There's no right-click and no double-tap in Time Slide. You never need them.

3Clocking on and off

The Dashboard shows one tile for each contract you work on.

Start work on a contract

Tap Start clock on its tile. The tile glows and the Current session time counts up.

Move to another contract

Just tap Start clock on the next tile. Only one clock runs at a time, so the first one stops and saves by itself.

Stop

Tap Stop clock. The time is saved straight away.

Rework

If the job is rework, tap Rework on the tile first and pick the reason. The button changes to Start rework. Tap it to start.

Time not on a contract

Use Start on the orange Non-contract box at the top of the Dashboard.

Breaks and the end of the day

  • A running clock pauses for your breaks and restarts afterwards by itself. While it's paused, the tile says On break. If you're not going back to that job, tap Stop clock.
  • If you forget to stop at the end of the day, Time Slide stops the clock for you (an auto clock-out). Check that day's end time next time you go to Edit times.

4Your tiles

Add a contract tileTap + Add tile, search for the contract number or customer, tick it, then tap Add to dashboard.
Move a tileHold a tile still until it buzzes and lifts, then slide it to its new place and let go. A quick swipe just scrolls the page.
Remove a tileTap the ⊗ at its bottom left. The contract's time is kept, and you can add the tile back later.

5Correcting your times

Tap Edit or Commit times in the menu. Each contract you worked on that day has a row, and each piece of time is a coloured block on its timeline.

Choose the day

Tap a day in the strip at the top. Swipe the strip to see earlier days, and tap Today to come back. The little bar under each day shows how full it is. A padlock means the day is committed.

Look along the day

Swipe the timeline left and right. Use − and + at the top to zoom. Zoom in when you need to set exact minutes.

Change a start or finish time

Put your finger on the end of a block and slide it. A label above your finger shows the time. Where two blocks meet, they move together, so there's no gap or overlap.

To move one end on its own, hold that end still until it turns into a red bar (it's now unlinked). Hold it again to link it back.

Add time you forgot to clock

  1. If the contract has no row that day, tap + Add contract and choose it.
  2. On an empty part of its row, hold still until the tablet buzzes, then slide along to draw the time. Let go when the label shows the right times.
  3. For rework, tap the row's Rework button and pick the reason before you draw.

Time you add this way gets a small pencil mark. You can add time to the last 28 days, but never past the current time. If you draw over other time, Time Slide asks before it takes that time away from the other block.

See, change or remove a block

Tap a block to see its times and any notes (department, rework reason, added by hand, auto clock-out).

For more, hold the middle of a block (not its ends) until the tablet buzzes. A menu opens with Change department… and Delete. Tap anywhere else to close it. Delete doesn't ask first: use Undo if you didn't mean it.

Check an auto clock-out

A block ended by an auto clock-out has a small clock badge. Tap the badge to keep that end time, or slide the end to when you really finished.

Made a mistake?

Use the Undo (↶) and Redo (↷) buttons beside Commit.

6Committing your day

When a day's times are right, tap Commit on Edit times and confirm.

Committing is final. Nobody can change a committed day afterwards, not even an administrator. Check every block first, especially any auto clock-outs.

If you have days you haven't committed, you may get an email reminder. Its link opens the first one for you.
